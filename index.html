Diversity-preserving search for biological discovery

I build quality-diversity frameworks and validation benchmarks that test whether these methods recover transcription factor binding motifs and other regulatory signals that single-objective search misses. I am also interested in hybrid machine learning models, robotics, and nature-inspired optimization.
